Takeaways

Strategy is stewardship

Choices are acts of faithful management of people, resources, and influence.

Alignment is measurable

Compare what an organization says with what its decisions reinforce.

Cases need structure

Define the problem, use evidence, recommend actions with consequences.

Leadership is behavior

Effective leadership shows up in specific, repeatable actions.
